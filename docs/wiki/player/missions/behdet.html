Briefing

On load the script shows message_soldiers_and_forts. Campaign history is message_history_behdet:

Egypt's military troops are now unmatched in the known world, but the new Pharaoh, Khasekhemwy of the second dynasty, now also demands a powerful navy based at Behdet. Nothing less than a fleet of warships will suffice to allow us dominion over the high seas, but timber is scarce, as our climate supports only a few sparse areas of forest. Cedar may be imported at great cost from Byblos, in the land of Lebanon to the northeast. Fortunately, exports of our native papyrus will provide a means of offsetting this expense.

Behdet (Edfu) is Upper Egypt’s navy mission: docks, warship wharves, shipwrights, and cedar from Byblos. Kushite raids pressure the land army while mastabas cover the monument rating.

Strategy

Open sea trade for timber (Byblos) and papyrus export partners; build a dock early. Keep forts and a weaponsmith ahead of year-5 Kushites. Stock beer for Pharaoh’s year-2 request to unlock the bricks gift for mastabas.

Phase 1 — City and docks

Food from chickpea / barley farms and fishing wharves. Place Shipwright, Dock, and Warship Wharves on navigable Nile water. Import copper if needed for spears.

Phase 2 — Trade and navy

Open Byblos (1 000 Db) for cedar; Men-nefer / Perwadjyt for sea goods; Nekhen (cheap land, 300 Db) and Timna for extras. Export papyrus and reeds to fund timber.

Phase 3 — Defense and monuments

Recruiter + infantry/archer forts before the first Kushite wave. Use gifted or produced bricks with Bricklayers Guild for small/medium mastabas toward monument 11.

Available buildings

Military, navy, mastabas, Osiris/Ra temples, pottery/beer/papyrus/bricks industry, and standard civic buildings are available from the start (see buildings[] in the script).
