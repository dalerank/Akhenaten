Briefing

The mission opens with the history panel (message_history_on) and the briefing popup (message_mission_heliopolis):

Pharaoh Khufu has taken the throne, and our people have already begun to suffer under his oppression. It is rumored he plans a colossal building project on the plateau outside Rostja, to usurp the renown his father Snofru earned from his two noble pyramids.

Khufu has ordered a cluster of quarries dug at Tura, where rich deposits of fine white limestone have been discovered. You must also build three mastaba tombs for the nobles of this region. The quarrying settlement you found shall be named "On", and shall serve as a plentiful source of limestone for many years.

The finest ivory may be obtained via Byblos, trading with Assyria and Ur. With the arrival of such rare luxury goods, On is sure to prosper.

On is the ancient Egyptian name for Heliopolis (Iunu). In the campaign it is the peaceful, high-culture arm of the Khufu fork: a limestone-quarrying town that builds three noble mastabas. Pak has no timed enemy raids — only a favour Pharaoh chain if kingdom rating collapses. Luxury goods come via Saqqara (Byblos is display-only foreign on this empire map).

Strategy

On trades military pressure for economic and cultural demands: culture 40, three mastabas, and a limestone-export economy. Keep kingdom rating above zero — favour is five waves of size 20.

Phase 1 — Limestone and food

Limestone Quarries supply mastabas, the recurring Pharaoh ask, and exports. Open Dahshur early (cheap, sea 200) for straw/beer; Saqqara for luxury goods.

Phase 2 — Three mastabas

Small mastabas need bricks — Clay Pits, Bricks Workshop, straw. Work Camps and guilds keep construction moving. Hunting Lodge for the perfect-flood game-meat chain.

Phase 3 — Culture 40 and prosperity 35

Scribal Schools, Libraries, temples (Osiris / Ra / Bast), full entertainment including Senet Houses. Import luxury via Saqqara.

Tips

  • No timed invasions — forts are optional insurance against favour collapse.
  • Size limestone production above the ×25 / 36mo ask plus exports.
  • Bast temples raise culture in a peaceful showcase city.
