Briefing

History panel (message_history_iunet) and briefing popup (message_mission_dendera):

Pharaoh Khufu has taken the throne, and, as foretold by the seers of Horus and Ra, our people have already begun to suffer under his oppression. Khufu has ordered that a Royal Governor be dispatched at once to Iunet, to defend our land against Kushite invaders.

Iunet can support a small fishing industry. If the Kushites invade by water, the river may become unsafe for fishing boats, and shore areas may be better used to support a fleet of warships. If food becomes scarce, cattle may be raised for meat, though the herds require much straw for fodder — straw you will also need to make bricks for your mastaba.

The finest ivory may be obtained via Byblos, trading with Assyria and Ur. And Pharaoh has ordered countless quarries dug at On — it is rumored he plans a colossal building project on the plateau outside Rostja.

Pak vs briefing: scenario enemy is Hittite (ENEMY_4_HITTITE), not Kushite. Briefing text still mentions Kushites.

Strategy

Population 4 000, housing 16, and escalating Hittite pressure around a single mastaba. Open Dahshur for straw; Byblos for luxury/timber.

Phase 1 — Food

Fishing for the year-1 fish ask; cattle for meat ×2. Import straw from Dahshur.

Phase 2 — Mastaba and exports

Granite quarry + gold mines; small mastaba rating 9. Pottery industry before y5.

Phase 3 — Defense

Hittite waves at y4 / y5m10 / y8 / y16. Keep walls, academy, and companies. Sea invasion points exist (naval landings still land-proxied).

Tips

  • Do not let kingdom rating hit zero — favour chain is four waves.
  • Stock limestone before y10.
  • Bast temples are available (pak gods Ra/Seth/Bast only).

Available buildings

All buildings are available from mission start. Religion: Ra, Seth, Bast (no Osiris/Ptah).
