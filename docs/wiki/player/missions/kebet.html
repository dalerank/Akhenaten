Scripted events (summary)

Food-request ladder (figs → fish → figs) plus weapons and henna asks. Timed Egyptian loyalist invasions (17 / 33 / 55 / 70). Henna refuse → egypt ×60 wipe chain (JS). Favour Pharaoh army ×60→×60 (land points 2 → 4). It is the OG kingdom army: when the kingdom rating falls to 0, the engine sends the first favour invasion — announcement and reminders first, the attack 9 months later; chained waves follow as soon as the previous wave attacks. It is called off if the rating climbs back to 15 before it attacks, and can come again 12 months later while the rating stays at 0. Failed / perfect floods with demand and price side effects. NEW_TRADE unlocks Men-nefer / Khmun mid-game.
