Scripted events (summary)

Gifts (copper / beer); gamemeat; oil → dual LOST cascades (ok: Waset/Men-nefer/Dunqul → pottery×23 → NEW×3; refuse: Henen-nesw/Sauty/Khmun) → egypt×38 → pottery×39; henna tribute / later henna; Khmun siege → troops ×48; timed + recurring Kushite raids; Egyptian chain invasions on request outcomes; favour Egyptian army ×50 → ×50 (land points 2 → 3). It is the OG kingdom army: when the kingdom rating falls to 0, the engine sends the first favour invasion — announcement and reminders first, the attack 9 months later; chained waves follow as soon as the previous wave attacks. It is called off if the rating climbs back to 15 before it attacks, and can come again 12 months later while the rating stays at 0.
