Briefing

The mission opens with the history panel (message_history_rostja) and the briefing popup (message_mission_giza):

Pharaoh Khufu has at last made his plans known. You have been awarded the status of Nomarch, but also charged with carrying out the most ambitious building project ever undertaken in our land.

Pharaoh's eternal resting place shall be a massive pyramid complex on the plateau outside Rostja. His sarcophagus shall be of solid granite, his funeral barge of precious Lebanese cedar. Beside it, a smaller pyramid shall be built for his son prince Khafra, who also declares his image be carved into the living rock as a "sphinx" — the body of a lion and the head of a man.

You'll need a large settlement to support the effort. Conditions there may not be overly refined; your goal is only to complete these three great projects, and to honor Pharaoh — whatever the cost.

Rostja (Rosetau) is the ancient name for the Giza plateau. This is the campaign's marquee monument mission — no population, culture, or prosperity requirement — only monuments, housing level, and kingdom rating. Empire id 18; scenario enemy Hyksos.

Strategy

Rostja is a monument grind with Hyksos pressure and a gems/timber import economy. Keep kingdom rating above zero — favour is three waves (50/20/50).

Phase 1 — Stone and trade

Open On early (sea 500) for limestone and meat. Byblos (1250) supplies cedar timber for burial provisions and the funeral barge. Fulfill the year-2 gems ask to unlock Men-nefer.

Phase 2 — Monuments

Lay out the stand-in stepped pyramids and mastabas early. Work Camps + all three guilds; granite/limestone/stone pipelines. Dispatch burial goods (beer, gems, luxury, timber, granite) via the Overseer of Monuments.

Phase 3 — Defense

Hyksos×6 at y5m7, crisis×11 after a failed gems ask, troops ask at y8, and recurring×10 from y26. Wall the plateau approaches and keep an academy-trained army.

Tips

  • No culture/prosperity goals — pour everything into stone and kingdom rating.
  • Gems exports/imports matter: Pharaoh recurring asks and the Byblos route both need them.
  • Keep kingdom above 0; favour waves are large (50).
