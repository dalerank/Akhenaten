Fog of war

Optional Enhanced feature: the part of the map your city cannot see is dark, and land you have never seen is black. The original Pharaoh has no fog of war, so the default is OFF; it is also not switched on by --enhanced.

What it does

  1. Enable Fog of war under Features → New features. It takes effect on the next game tick (unpause to see it).
  2. At the start of a mission (or the first time you turn it on in a running city) the land around the land and river entry points is known, and so is everything your buildings and walkers see.
  3. Unexplored land is black: no terrain, no figures, no tooltip, no info window. The minimap shows it black too.
  4. Fogged land (seen before, not watched now) is drawn darker. Terrain stays as it is, but enemies, animals, foreign traders and their ships are not shown there, on the map or the minimap.
  5. Visible land is drawn as usual; a thin darker rim marks the edge of what your city sees.

Turning the feature off shows the whole map again and keeps what you explored, so turning it back on restores it. Some missions may switch fog off for themselves.

Sending soldiers out is how you explore far land; the sight updates ten times a day.

Building

You can only build on explored land. A footprint, a house plot or a road, canal or wall drag that touches unexplored land is refused (You can only build on land you have explored). A new building lights its surroundings at once, even while the game is paused.

What stays as in Pharaoh

  • The city simulates exactly the same with fog on or off: no walker, enemy or random decision looks at the fog.
  • Messages keep their locations (invasions, fires, collapses, predators), and the advisors report enemy numbers as before.
  • Sounds of figures in the fog are still heard.
  • A figure standing in the fog still blocks building on its tile.
  • Legion, warship and transport orders into fog or unexplored land are allowed.
Developer Reference

Flag: gameplay_enhanced_fog_of_war (game_feature::not_in_enhanced)

State and sim step: src/city/city_fog_of_war.cpp, save section fog_of_war (explored tiles only).

Config (sight radii, update rate, masks): src/scripts/fog_of_war.js

Per mission: an optional fog_of_war { enabled, initial_reveal_radius, reveal: [ { hint, tile, radius, from, to } ] } block in the mission script.

Drawing: src/widget/city/fog_tile.h (tile tint carried on each render record), figures gated in src/widget/widget_city.cpp, minimap in src/widget/widget_minimap.cpp.

Placement gate: src/building/construction/placement_checks.cpp, routed.cpp, build_planner.cpp.
