Mausoleum

The Mausoleum is a sandstone burial monument on clear land. Footprint 8×22 (rotates to 22×8). Guilds: Work Camp (clear + haul), Stonemasons (two stories), and Carpenters (wooden ramps between stories). Not Artisans. Three visual skins share the same gameplay (My Palace / mission pick). Finished mausoleums are burial tombs (provisions + funeral).

Placement

  • Requires 240 sandstone in staffed Storage Yards (#mausoleum_needs_sandstone / gr19:188).
  • Sandstone is consumed on successful place.
  • Only one unfinished mausoleum at a time.
  • Entire 8×22 (or 22×8) footprint must be clear land.
  • Three art skins (same size): cycle with Ctrl+R before place (titles gr198:25/26/27).

Construction

  • Work Camp peasants clear and level the site.
  • Stonemasons build the first story (sandstone haul).
  • Carpenters raise wooden ramps (timber).
  • Stonemasons complete the second story.
  • Haul mid-build when a Storage Yard holds ≥4 sandstone blocks (peasant sledge).

Missions

Primary: 30 Heh (monuments goal 15 = weight 5 + one burial point). Also Alexandria₁ (48, goal 14), Baki/Sawu/Rowarty/Hetep/Alexandria₃.
