Caesareum Cleo

The Caesareum is Alexandria’s seaside temple complex: a central marble temple, paved patio, surrounding portico, and two granite obelisks at the entrance. It is built from nine 5×5 sections in a 15×15 square centred on the tile you click. Stonemasons lay white marble that Work Camp peasants haul from a Storage Yard; Carpenters raise scaffolding; the last stage needs granite.

Placement

  • The whole 15×15 square must be free: shrubs, meadow and groundwater are fine; trees, roads, buildings, water, rocks and floodplain are not. Keep 3 tiles from the map edge.
  • Only one Caesareum per city (#one_caesareum_only).
  • Placing it warns when no marble is stored, or when no granite is quarried or stored.

Construction

  • 19 stages (0–18). Each stage has one stonemason task; stages 2–6 and 9–11 also have a carpenter task. Only one worker is on site at a time; the stage ends when all its tasks are done.
  • An arriving stonemason waits at the entrance until peasants bring two sleds of marble (granite in stage 18), 4 blocks per sled, then works his task.
  • The entrance (and the work tile) faces the camera, so it moves when you rotate the view.
  • Each stage shows its own art (caesareum1…caesareum20); progress is 5% per stage.
