Abu Simbel Cleo

Abu Simbel is the Cleopatra cliff-carved monument of four seated colossi. The bulk of the footprint sits on elevation (cliffs); a 3×3 entrance juts onto clear land. Construction uses timber scaffolding with Carpenters, then Stonemasons carve top-down. There is no Work Camp, no Artisans guild, and no stone delivery onto the monument — the cliff is the stone. Do not confuse with unused enum BUILDING_COLOSSI (=270) — that is only an editor/win label, not this building.

Placement

  • Bulk tiles must sit on elevation / access ramp (cliffs). Use Hide Cliffs (H) to inspect.
  • The centered 3×3 entrance protrudes past the far depth edge onto clear land (total depth 24 at rot 0/2).
  • Only one Abu Simbel on the map (finished still blocks a second).
  • Cannot be demolished (clear land is blocked; shows a warning).
  • Stone in storage is not required to place (unlike Sun Temple sandstone).

Construction

  • Early phases: deliver timber and raise scaffolding (carpenters).
  • Later phases: mason-only carve; paint is baked into finished art (no Artisans).
  • Art layers from AbuSimbel.sg3: progressive 2statue façade, midcut niche, scaffold overlays, entrance stairs.
  • Exact timber loads per phase are still provisional (Heaven chart only marks wood = Y; no counts in pack/exe).

Missions

Required on mission 43 · Abu Simbel (monuments goal 44 — one finished Abu Simbel satisfies the criterion).
