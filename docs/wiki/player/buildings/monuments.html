Monuments

Overview

Monuments are large multi-phase construction projects. Many campaign missions require a Monuments rating high enough to finish the city; some also demand a specific monument type. Construction depends on raw materials (bricks, stone, timber, and later marble or sandstone), Work Camps for peasant labour, and the right construction guilds to direct the work.

Detailed pages for individual monuments will appear here as they are documented. Currently: Sphinx, Obelisk.

Size and materials charts adapted from Pharaoh Heaven — Monuments and the Pharaoh/Cleopatra Monuments Chart.

How construction works

Place site → Stock materials → Work Camps + guilds → Phases / courses → Finished monument
  1. Place the footprint on clear ground (or cliffs / rock where the monument requires it).
  2. Deliver materials to storage yards near the site. Quarries and brickworks are often the bottleneck.
  3. Staff Work Camps so labourers walk to the monument. The same camps also serve farms — harvest season can starve construction.
  4. Staff the required guilds (bricklayers, stonemasons, carpenters, and in Cleopatra artisans). Guild members supervise on site.
  5. Work proceeds in phases (mastaba courses, pyramid courses, scaffolding, finishing). Progress shows in the monument info window.
  6. After a burial tomb is finished and burial provisions are dispatched (Overseer of Monuments; skip if the mission requires none), a one-shot funeral walker procession walks to the tomb and despawns. Tomb robbers stealing provisions later do not repeat the funeral.
  7. Dispatched goods credit a per-tomb stock ledger (emptiest finished tomb first). City totals still drive the Overseer UI and win checks; robbers empty the target tomb’s stock, then the city counter. With several tombs, one site can be looted without wiping every grave.
Monuments do not need a road connection. Place them where materials and labour can reach them reasonably quickly — not across the entire map from your quarries and camps.

Guilds & labour

BuildingRole
Work Camp Peasant labour for clearing, hauling, and building courses. Shared with floodplain farming.
Bricklayers’ Guild Brick mastabas and brick-core pyramids.
Stonemasons’ Guild Stone tombs, stepped / true / bent pyramids, sphinx, obelisks, mausoleum, sun temple, most Cleopatra monuments.
Carpenters’ Guild Wooden scaffolding and ramps (pyramids, sphinx, mausoleum, obelisks, etc.).
Artisans’ Guild Cleo Painting and finishing for royal burial tombs and several Cleopatra monuments.

You often need more than one guild of the same type on large monuments. Keep storage yards supplied; idle guilds with empty yards do nothing.

Cleopatra monuments

The Cleopatra expansion adds cliff tombs and Alexandria-era monuments. Materials vary (marble, sandstone, timber, copper, granite); guilds are usually Work Camp + Carpenters + Stonemasons — Artisans mainly for royal tombs. Some monuments must sit on cliffs or a special island.

MonumentFootprintNotes
Royal Burial Tomb Cleo 11×20 … 23×29 Cliff tombs; lamps + paint; Artisans + Work Camps; burial ledger + robbers
Abu Simbel Cleo 9×21 Bulk in cliffs, entrance on clear land; four colossi; Stonemasons + Carpenters (+ timber); weight 48
Great Library of Alexandria Cleo 13×14 Marble + timber + copper; WC + Carpenters + Stonemasons; weight 12
Pharos Lighthouse Cleo 6×6 Rocky island only; marble ~108 + timber; WC + Carpenters + Stonemasons; weight 8; not demolishable
Caesareum Cleo 15×15 Clear land, free; 19 stages; marble 144 + granite 8 (obelisks); WC + Carpenters + Stonemasons; weight 10

Monument rating

Finished (and partially built) monuments contribute to the city’s Monuments rating. Akhenaten uses the original formula from src/scripts/city/monuments.js: only the monuments the scenario asks for count — each of its up to three monument slots takes the first matching monument built in this city (one carried over from an earlier mission takes none) — and each adds trunc(weight × progress% / 100) points; each required burial provision adds trunc(dispatched / divisor) (divisor 32 for grain, game meat, gems, timber and the stones; 16 for weapons, pottery, beer, linen, luxury goods, papyrus and chariots), and rating = trunc(6.32 × √points + 0.5), one less while a slot monument is unfinished, clamped to 0–100. The square root means each extra monument adds less than the one before.

Weights: mastaba S/M/L 2 / 3 / 11; stepped pyramid S/M/L 3 / 9 / 20, complex 36, grand complex 60; mudbrick and true pyramid S/M/L 4 / 12 / 26, complex 48, grand complex 80; bent pyramid S/M 4 / 10; Sphinx 10; obelisk small 1 / large 3; Sun Temple 4; Mausoleum 5; royal tomb S/M/L/G 4 / 12 / 26 / 48; Abu Simbel 48; Pharos 8; Library 12; Caesareum 10. Examples: one finished small mastaba → 9; three → 15; one medium stepped pyramid → 19.

Tips

  • Start material production early — stone and bricks take longer than the build phases themselves.
  • Build one monument first; when a course finishes and labour frees up, start the next.
  • During floodplain harvest, expect Work Camp labour to thin out; stockpile stone beforehand.
  • Place storage yards and guilds near the site so cart pushers and guild walkers waste less time.
  • Import granite, limestone, or sandstone when the map has no matching quarry.
