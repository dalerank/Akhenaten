Pharos Lighthouse Cleo

The Pharos Lighthouse is Alexandria’s three-tier beacon on rocky ground (Pharos Island). Guilds: Work Camp (clear), Stonemasons (marble tiers), and Carpenters (scaffolding). Not Artisans. Materials: imported white marble (~108) and timber. Finished Pharos cannot be demolished.

Placement

  • Entire 6×6 footprint must be rocky ground (all green). Not clear land / water.
  • Only one unfinished Pharos at a time (#one_pharos_only).
  • Finished: not demolishable (#pharos_not_demolishable).
  • Marble haul from a Storage Yard with ≥4 blocks in one yard (help).

Construction

  • Work Camp peasants clear the rock pad.
  • Stonemasons: foundation → floor → square tier → octagonal tier → cupola.
  • Carpenters raise and later remove timber scaffolding.
  • Art packs: LtHouse1…LtHouse9 (beacon LtHouse10 optional).

Missions

Consumer mission 49 (A Beacon of Light / Ptolemy's Alexandria) — with Library; sandbox: m_128_alexandria.js.
